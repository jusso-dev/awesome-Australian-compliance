# Glossary

Short definitions for terms used across the pack. Where a term has a precise statutory meaning
the entry points to the legislation rather than restating it.

## A

- **ACSC** - Australian Cyber Security Centre, the part of the Australian Signals Directorate
  responsible for cyber.gov.au, the Essential Eight, the ISM and the IRAP programme.
- **APP** - Australian Privacy Principle. Thirteen principles in Schedule 1 of the Privacy Act
  1988 that bind APP entities.
- **APP entity** - An agency or organisation covered by the Privacy Act 1988. Most
  Commonwealth agencies and most private-sector organisations with annual turnover above
  AUD 3 million.
- **APRA** - Australian Prudential Regulation Authority. Regulates banks, insurers and
  superannuation funds. Publishes CPS 234 on information security.
- **ASD** - Australian Signals Directorate, parent of the ACSC.
- **Assessment Process Guide (APG)** - ACSC's published methodology for assessing Essential
  Eight maturity.

## C

- **CDR** - Consumer Data Right. The framework that requires data holders in banking, energy
  and other regulated sectors to share customer data with accredited data recipients.
- **CPS 234** - APRA Prudential Standard CPS 234 Information Security.

## D

- **DSS** - Digital Service Standard, published by the Digital Transformation Agency.
- **DTA** - Digital Transformation Agency.

## E

- **Essential Eight (E8)** - Eight mitigation strategies published by the ACSC, each with
  three maturity levels.

## I

- **IRAP** - Information Security Registered Assessors Program. ACSC programme that registers
  independent assessors who evaluate systems against the ISM.
- **ISM** - Information Security Manual, published by the ACSC. The Australian government's
  primary cyber security control catalogue.

## M

- **Maturity Level (ML)** - The Essential Eight Maturity Model defines ML1, ML2 and ML3,
  representing increasing levels of mitigation strength against tradecraft of increasing
  sophistication.

## N

- **NDB scheme** - Notifiable Data Breaches scheme under Part IIIC of the Privacy Act 1988.

## O

- **OAIC** - Office of the Australian Information Commissioner. The regulator for the Privacy
  Act 1988.
- **OFFICIAL / OFFICIAL: Sensitive / PROTECTED / SECRET / TOP SECRET** - Australian
  government information classifications. This pack covers up to PROTECTED.

## P

- **PIA** - Privacy Impact Assessment.
- **PSPF** - Protective Security Policy Framework, published by the Attorney-General's
  Department.

## S

- **SoA** - Statement of Applicability. Lists which controls apply to a system and which do
  not, with justifications.
- **SSP** - System Security Plan. Describes how a system implements its applicable controls.

## T

- **TGA** - Therapeutic Goods Administration. Regulates software as a medical device in
  Australia.
