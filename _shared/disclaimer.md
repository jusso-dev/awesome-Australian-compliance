# Disclaimer

This repository is general information for software engineers, security practitioners and
people preparing for compliance work in Australia. It is published to help AI coding agents
do useful work alongside humans.

It is not legal advice. It is not a privacy impact assessment. It is not an IRAP assessment
and it is not signed off by an IRAP-registered assessor.

If you are:

- Preparing a system for an IRAP assessment, engage an [IRAP-registered
  assessor](https://www.cyber.gov.au/about-us/about-asd/programs-and-services/irap) before
  relying on anything in this repo as evidence.
- Responding to a notifiable data breach under the Privacy Act 1988, get legal advice and
  contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
- Making a decision about classification, data residency or cross-border disclosure, talk to
  your agency security adviser or your legal team.

Contents are drawn from publicly available primary sources (cyber.gov.au, oaic.gov.au,
protectivesecurity.gov.au, apra.gov.au, dta.gov.au, legislation.gov.au and equivalent state
government domains). Frameworks change. Always confirm the live published version before
relying on a control reference.

The maintainers accept no liability for decisions made on the basis of this content.
