# ISM guideline to ISO/IEC 27001 mapping (cell references only)

This table maps ISM guideline groups to the most closely related ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex
A control category. It exists for the common case of mapping an ISM-aligned posture to
ISO 27001 for non-Australian clients or vendors.

This pack does not paraphrase ISO 27001. The table below is a cell-reference mapping
only. Use your licensed copy of ISO 27001 for the wording of each Annex A control.

## How to use

- Use the table to identify the cluster of ISO 27001 Annex A controls that an ISM-aligned
  posture is likely to address. Then, with a licensed copy of ISO 27001, verify whether
  the specific controls are satisfied.
- The mapping is at the category level, not at the individual ISM control level.
  Individual ISM controls within a guideline may map to ISO controls in other categories.
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022 is the implementation-guidance companion to ISO 27001 and is often
  the better cross-reference for understanding intent. Confirm against your licensed copy.
