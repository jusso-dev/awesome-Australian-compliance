# PSPF policy to ISM guideline mapping

This table maps the Protective Security Policy Framework (PSPF) policies to the ISM
guidelines that contain the related technical controls. The PSPF is administered by the
Attorney-General's Department; the ISM is administered by the ACSC. The two frameworks
are designed to interlock at the agency level.

Phase 1 of this pack ships the mapping table only. The PSPF skill ships in a later phase
with per-policy detail.

## PSPF policy index (summary)

The PSPF is organised into Core Requirements, grouped under four outcomes: Governance,
Information Security, Personnel Security and Physical Security. Each Core Requirement
is supported by a numbered Policy.

## How to use

- For each PSPF policy applicable to the system, identify the ISM guidelines that hold
  the related technical controls. Then walk those guideline pages for codebase signals
  and evidence types.
- PSPF compliance is reported annually by Commonwealth agencies. The ISM is the
  technical control catalogue underpinning much of that compliance, particularly for
  Policies 10 and 11.
