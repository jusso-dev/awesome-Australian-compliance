# Stage 1 organisational intake and pre-engagement

The ACSC's **IRAP Common Assessment Framework (CAF) v1.0, April 2025** sets out the
inputs the assessor requires before the assessment formally begins. This page maps
those inputs to the practical intake an assessee can prepare in advance.

> Source: ASD, IRAP Common Assessment Framework. Last-verified: 2026-05-13.
> <https://www.cyber.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-04/IRAP%20common%20assessment%20framework.pdf>

This page covers two layers:

1. **CAF-required Stage 1 inputs.** Items the CAF explicitly identifies for Stage 1
   (Plan and prepare). Reference: CAF section "Stage 1: Plan and prepare" and the IRAP
   Assessment Requirements IRAP-AR-0008 through IRAP-AR-0016.
2. **Common-practice intake.** Items not strictly required by the CAF but standard in
   commercial IRAP engagements (organisational profile, logo for the report cover,
   ABN / ACN, mission statement). Useful for the engagement letter and for the report's
   front matter under the ASD IRAP Assessment Report Template.

## CAF Stage 1 inputs - assessor side

The CAF binds the assessor to submit an **engagement form** through the ASD IRAP
**Partner Portal**. The form includes a **Conflict of Interest (COI) declaration**.
Hard requirements:

- **IRAP-AR-0008** - The assessor submits a COI declaration to ASD IRAP prior to
  commencing the assessment.
- **IRAP-AR-0009** - The COI declaration is submitted at least **7 business days**
  prior to commencement.
- **IRAP-AR-0010** - The assessor maintains the COI declaration throughout the
  engagement and informs ASD IRAP of changes.
- **IRAP-AR-0011** - The report outlines all conflicts of interest for authorising
  officers.

In consultation with the client, the assessor must also determine:

- **Assessment dates**: start date, expected end date, milestones.
- **Access to resources**: documentation, system accounts, tools, personnel,
  facilities.
- **Methodologies**: sampling, evidence gathering, testing approach.
- **System and control testing activities** that will be conducted.
- **Evidence collection processes and protection**, including post-assessment
  requirements.
- **Outsourcing**: whether managed service providers or other outsourced providers
  manage any aspect, with points of contact.
- **Applicable frameworks**: HCF (Hosting Certification Framework), SCEC (Security
  Construction and Equipment Committee) assessment, PSPF.
- **Use and marketing of the IRAP assessment and artefacts** - what the report can and
  cannot be used for.
- **ASD QA availability** of IRAP artefacts.
- **Team composition** - assessor plus supporting security assessment team where
  required (IRAP-AR-0016).

The CAF notes the assessor **may develop an IRAP assessment plan** documenting these
items and share it with the client. This is the canonical CAF artefact for Stage 1.

## CAF Stage 1 inputs - assessee side

The CAF lists the documents the assessor will request from the assessed entity during
Stage 1:

- **System Security Plan (SSP) and annex.**
- **Risk management documents** (security risk management plan, risk register).
- **Design and architectural documents.**
- **Incident response plans and playbooks.**
- **Organisational policies.**
- **Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).**
- **Security test cases and test plans.**
- **Business Continuity Plans (BCP)** and **Disaster Recovery Plans (DRP).**
- **Functional and non-functional requirements.**
- **Configuration and build documents.**
- **DevSecOps documents** (including pipeline).

If any of these does not exist or is significantly out of date, address that before
the COI clock starts.

## Common-practice intake (not strictly CAF-required)

The following items are standard in commercial IRAP engagements but not enumerated as
CAF Stage 1 inputs. They typically appear in the engagement letter and the report's
front matter (the ASD IRAP Assessment Report Template's cover sheet).

### Entity profile

- **Legal name** of the entity being assessed.
- **Trading name(s)** if different.
- **Entity type** (Commonwealth non-corporate, corporate Commonwealth, state agency,
  local government, private-sector, other).
- **ABN** and **ACN** where applicable.
- **Registered office address** and operational addresses.
- **Website URL.**
- **Logo** in vector format for the report cover.
- **Mission / purpose** in one or two paragraphs.
- **Services in scope of the system being assessed.**
- **Approximate headcount.**

### Principal contacts

A roster captured up front saves cycles during the engagement. Names, roles, phone
numbers and email addresses for:

- **Accountable authority** (agency head or equivalent).
- **Chief Security Officer (CSO)** or equivalent.
- **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** if separate from CSO.
- **System owner** for the system in scope.
- **Privacy officer** where personal information is in scope.
- **Engagement sponsor** within the entity.
- **Engagement point of contact** day-to-day.
- **Technical SMEs** by domain: identity, network, endpoint, application, cloud,
  monitoring, incident response.
- **Communications point of contact** for external messaging during the engagement.
- **Legal counsel** primary contact.

### Engagement parameters (common, not from the CAF directly)

- **Authorising agency** (the agency that will rely on the IRAP outcome).
- **Existing IRAP history** (prior assessments, dates, scope changes since).
- **Engagement classification** - the classification of the assessment workpapers
  themselves, typically matching the system's ceiling.
- **Information-handling agreement** for the assessor's working papers.
- **Document exchange channel** (the assessor's secure portal, a shared workspace).

## Pre-engagement screening (common-practice questions)

The CAF does not publish a fixed prescreening question set. The questions below are
standard items commercial assessors raise before the engagement starts. They draw on
the CAF's Stage 1 requirements (especially IRAP-AR-0008 through -0011 on conflict of
interest) and on practical engagement-management experience.

1. **Authorisation to engage.** Is the engagement approved by the accountable
   authority or their delegate? Provide the record.
2. **Conflict of interest (entity side).** Any prior commercial or personal
   relationships between the entity and the proposed assessor in the last three years?
3. **Conflict of interest (assessor side).** Confirm the assessor's COI declaration is
   submitted to ASD IRAP at least 7 business days before commencement (IRAP-AR-0009).
4. **Capacity.** Does the entity have the personnel and access to documentation to
   support the proposed schedule? Named SMEs and their availability.
5. **Prior assessments.** Any prior IRAP, ISO 27001, SOC 2 or other assessments
   covering substantially the same system? Provide the report(s).
6. **Outstanding remediations.** Any findings from prior assessments not yet closed?
   Provide the register.
7. **Recent material change.** Has the system undergone material change in the last
   90 days?
8. **Known incidents.** Any reportable incidents in the last 12 months affecting the
   system? Brief description and state.
9. **Pending change.** Any material change planned during the engagement window? Agree
   to defer or include in scope.
10. **Sub-processors.** Any change to the sub-processor list since the most recent
    assessment?
11. **Personnel security.** Are personnel with access to engagement information
    cleared to the appropriate level? Provide roster (names redacted; clearance levels
    per role).
12. **Foreign relationships.** Foreign ownership, control or influence considerations?
13. **Legal exposure.** Any active litigation or regulatory investigation affecting
    the system?
14. **Insurance and indemnities.** Confirm the engagement contract addresses
    professional indemnity and liability.
15. **Branding.** Provide approved logo and branding for the report.
16. **Information handling.** Agree how the assessor stores, transmits and destroys
    the entity's information during and after the engagement.

## Outputs of intake

By the end of intake the entity has:

- A complete Stage 1 documentation pack matching the CAF list.
- A confirmed engagement schedule and milestones.
- A signed engagement letter
  ([`./engagement-letter-template.md`](./engagement-letter-template.md)).
- A completed prescreening questionnaire.
- Confirmation that the assessor's COI declaration has been submitted to ASD IRAP and
  the 7 business-day clock has elapsed.
- A confirmed information-handling agreement for the engagement workpapers.

## How an agent should use this page

When the user is preparing for an IRAP engagement:

1. Walk the **CAF Stage 1 inputs - assessee side** list and surface gaps in the user's
   documentation. Where the SSP, BCP, DRP, IR plan, SOPs, requirements documents, build
   documents or DevSecOps documentation is missing or stale, flag it and offer to
   scaffold from templates in this pack.
2. Walk the **Entity profile** and **Principal contacts** lists and produce a draft
   from what is visible in the codebase, the website, the IdP and the public records.
   Confirm with the user before publishing.
3. Walk the **Pre-engagement screening** questions and capture answers.
4. Pre-fill the **engagement letter** with the values gathered.
5. Confirm the **COI clock**. The assessor cannot start until 7 business days after
   submitting the declaration to ASD IRAP (IRAP-AR-0009).

## Common pitfalls

- Logo provided in raster only; the ASD IRAP Assessment Report Template cover wants
  vector.
- Engagement classified below the system's classification ceiling. If the SSP carries
  PROTECTED information, the engagement workpapers do too.
- Principal contacts list out of date.
- Prescreening done verbally and not written down.
- Sub-processor list assumed unchanged from the last engagement.
- Assessor proceeds before the COI declaration is acknowledged by ASD IRAP, violating
  IRAP-AR-0009.
