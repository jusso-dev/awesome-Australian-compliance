# Engagement letter - template

A drop-in engagement letter for an IRAP assessment. The engagement letter is
commercial paperwork between the assessor's firm and the assessed entity. It is **not**
a CAF artefact - the CAF's canonical Stage 1 artefact is the (optional) **IRAP
assessment plan** the assessor prepares - but most engagements use a letter alongside.

This template captures the intake outputs from
[`./organisational-intake.md`](./organisational-intake.md). Fill the bracketed
placeholders. Delete guidance in italics before signing.

## 5. Pre-engagement screening

The parties confirm the prescreening questionnaire has been completed and signed. See
[`./examples/prescreening-questionnaire.md`](./examples/prescreening-questionnaire.md)
for the form. The completed prescreening is attached as Appendix A.

The assessor confirms:

- The Conflict of Interest declaration has been submitted to ASD IRAP via the IRAP
  Partner Portal at least 7 business days before the commencement date stated in
  Section 4, satisfying **IRAP-AR-0008** and **IRAP-AR-0009**. The assessor will
  maintain and update the declaration throughout the engagement
  (**IRAP-AR-0010**) and the report will outline any conflicts for authorising
  officers (**IRAP-AR-0011**).
- No conflict of interest preventing this engagement, and independence requirements
  per the IRAP programme satisfied.
- Capacity to deliver the engagement within the agreed schedule, supported by a
  security assessment team of sufficient technical breadth (**IRAP-AR-0016**).

The assessed entity confirms:

- Engagement approved by the accountable authority or delegate.
- Personnel resourced to support the engagement.
- Disclosed all relevant prior assessments, outstanding remediations, recent material
  change, known incidents and planned change.
