# IRAP Assessment Requirements

The IRAP Common Assessment Framework (CAF) v1.0 defines 46 numbered IRAP Assessment
Requirements (IRAP-AR-0001 through IRAP-AR-0046), grouped under seven quality
standards. Every requirement was last updated April 2025 in v1.0.

This page is the reference matrix. The IRAP-ARs are quoted from the CAF; the
"applies to" column adds the CAF stage the requirement most clearly attaches to.

> Source: ASD, IRAP Common Assessment Framework v1.0, Appendix B - Assessment
> requirements matrix. Last-verified: 2026-05-13.
> <https://www.cyber.gov.au/sites/default/files/2025-04/IRAP%20common%20assessment%20framework.pdf>

## Seven quality standards

- **Report quality and terminology** - outputs are detailed, accurate and aligned to
  ASD's published guidance and frameworks.
- **Assessment process and frameworks** - the assessment follows the CAF and considers
  other Australian Government frameworks and policies.
- **Evidence gathering** - evidence is of sufficient quality and sampled appropriately
  for the system, control and timeframe.
- **Coverage** - sufficient coverage of the assessment; out-of-boundary components are
  clearly articulated and justified.
- **Objectivity** - findings are unbiased and substantiated with sufficient quality
  evidence at the time of the assessment.
- **Technical accuracy and completeness** - the assessor has a strong technical
  understanding of the system and can determine and articulate control effectiveness.
- **Assessment integrity** - independence is maintained and circumstances that could
  impede independence are reported.

## Matrix

### Report quality and terminology

| Requirement | Description | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IRAP-AR-0003 | The assessor uses the correct terminology and intent for assessing control implementation, defined by ASD. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0006 | The IRAP assessment report clearly articulates any constraints or limitations affecting the assessment, and their impacts. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0007 | The assessor gathers consumer recommendations from the provider, evaluates and includes them in the relevant section of the report. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0023 | The assessor only assesses what is implemented and not what will be implemented. The assessor may outline programs of work underway. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0032 | The assessment report and controls matrix are reviewed by security professionals internally and by the stakeholder prior to final release. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0033 | The assessor articulates the potential impact but does not rate risks on behalf of the assessed entity or government agency. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0034 | Key vulnerabilities and weaknesses are clearly articulated in the report as early as possible. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0044 | The report clearly outlines the shared responsibility model. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0045 | The assessor uses the ASD-provided IRAP assessment report and controls matrix templates, modifying sections as required. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0046 | The assessor provides a complete report and controls matrix to the assessed entity on completion. | Stage 4 |

### Assessment process and frameworks

| Requirement | Description | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IRAP-AR-0012 | The assessment uses necessary Australian Government cyber security frameworks, policies and guidance relevant to the entity and system, including but not limited to, the ISM and PSPF. | Stage 1 / Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0013 | The assessment report clearly articulates activities conducted during each stage of the assessment. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0014 | The assessor uses the latest release of the ISM available prior to the beginning of the assessment. | Stage 1 |
| IRAP-AR-0015 | Where the time taken to complete an assessment lapses 2 releases of the ISM, the assessor has conducted a delta assessment against the current version. | Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0022 | The assessment follows the methodologies and approaches outlined in the CAF; building upon the process where necessary. | All stages |
| IRAP-AR-0039 | The report outlines risk management processes the entity has used to manage risks and threats. | Stage 4 |

### Evidence gathering

| Requirement | Description | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IRAP-AR-0001 | The assessor has assessed all components within the assessment boundary and has used sampling only where appropriate, to determine control effectiveness. | Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0002 | The assessor clearly explains the sampling methodology, outlining advantages, disadvantages, and why it was chosen. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0005 | The assessor gathers evidence at sufficient quality and appropriate to the system and control being assessed. | Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0026 | The assessor outlines within the report and controls matrix the evidence gathered to support the implementation and ongoing maintenance of a control. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0027 | Where the assessor cannot obtain sufficient evidence, the limitations and impact are documented in the report and controls marked accordingly. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |

### Coverage

| Requirement | Description | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IRAP-AR-0017 | The assessor regularly reviews, validates and maintains the assessment boundary. | Stage 2 |
| IRAP-AR-0018 | The report clearly defines the assessment boundary. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0019 | The report covers data sovereignty, offshore equipment and staff, or any information (including metadata) that is not within Australia. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0020 | The assessment covers all applicable environments, software, workstations, network devices, servers and other devices or services within the boundary. | Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0021 | The rationale for controls, systems and architecture that are out of scope is clearly articulated in the report and controls matrix. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0029 | The assessor understands the previous IRAP report and outlines any relevant findings on the entity. | Stage 1 / Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0031 | When the assessment covers multiple services, each service is assessed against the controls applicable to that service. Deviations from common controls are clearly outlined per service. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |

### Objectivity

| Requirement | Description | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IRAP-AR-0004 | The assessor bases the assessment on presented evidence and facts and has not made inappropriate assumptions. | Stage 3 |
| IRAP-AR-0040 | The report and controls matrix clearly articulate why a control, design, process or procedure is effective or ineffective. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0041 | The assessor does not use statements of compliance, conformity, certification or authorisation. Such statements undermine the assessment's ability to support a risk-based authority-to-operate decision. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0042 | The report does not include biased or misleading statements or any marketing jargon. | Stage 4 |

### Technical accuracy and completeness

| Requirement | Description | Applies to |
| --- | --- | --- |

| IRAP-AR-0025 | The assessor demonstrates sound technical understanding of the system and testing controls, articulated through the report. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0028 | Where technology capabilities and services are used by the entity, an explanation of function and purpose is outlined in the report. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0030 | Alternate controls specified in the report and controls matrix effectively meet the intent of the ISM control. Use of the alternate control outcome must be supported by sufficient evidence. | Stage 3 / Stage 4 |

| IRAP-AR-0036 | The report is written in an appropriate manner for the intended audience. | Stage 4 |

| IRAP-AR-0038 | The report clearly explains system architecture, design and the implementation of security controls. | Stage 4 |
| IRAP-AR-0043 | Where the entity uses technical guidance, the report clearly details deviations from the guidance. | Stage 4 |

## How an agent should use this matrix

- When the user asks "is this IRAP-AR-XXXX satisfied", quote the requirement text from
  this matrix and walk the relevant stage's expectations from
  [`./assessment-workflow.md`](./assessment-workflow.md).
- When the user is producing artefacts (SSP, SoA, evidence pack, report response),
  cross-reference the IRAP-ARs that bind the artefact. Most assessee-side artefacts
  attach to Stage 1 (preparation) and Stage 3 (evidence gathering).
- When the user is reading a draft report from an assessor, the report-quality
  requirements (IRAP-AR-0006, -0023, -0032 through -0046) help frame whether the
  draft is complete.
