# APP 2 - Anonymity and pseudonymity

APP 2 gives individuals the option of dealing with an APP entity anonymously or under a
pseudonym, unless an exception applies.

## In plain English

Where reasonable, let people interact with you without giving their real identity. If you
need real identity, have a documented reason.

## Why it exists

APP 2 is a check on identity-by-default. It pushes entities to ask whether they really
need identification for the interaction in question.

## What APP 2 requires

- Individuals must have the option of dealing with the entity anonymously or by
  pseudonym in respect of a particular matter.
- The exceptions: where the entity is required or authorised by law or court order to
  deal with identified individuals, or where it is impracticable for the entity to deal
  with anonymous or pseudonymous individuals.

## Evidence

- Documented assessment of which transactions require identification and which do not.
- Customer-facing options for anonymous interaction (general enquiry forms, public
  reporting channels, complaint mechanisms).
- Pseudonym handling: where a system allows display names distinct from legal names.

## Where to look

- Customer-facing forms: where the form requires identification, look for justification.
- Account creation flows: separate the "create an account" path from the "ask a
  question" path.
- Complaint and enquiry channels.

## Common pitfalls

- Identification required everywhere "for fraud prevention" without a documented
  threat-and-control link.
- Pseudonyms allowed in the UI but joined to legal identity in the back end without
  disclosure.
- Mandatory identification for transactions that have a clear non-identified alternative
  in legislation.
