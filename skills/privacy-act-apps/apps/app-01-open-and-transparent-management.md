# APP 1 - Open and transparent management of personal information

APP 1 sets the baseline obligation: an APP entity must manage personal information openly
and transparently. The headline requirement is a clearly expressed and up-to-date privacy
policy describing how the entity handles personal information.

## In plain English

Have a privacy policy. Make it accurate. Make it findable. Cover what you collect, why,
how it's held, how to access it and complain about it, and any overseas disclosure.

## Why it exists

APP 1 underpins trust. Individuals can only exercise rights under the other APPs if they
know what an entity does with personal information.

## What APP 1 requires

- Manage personal information in an open and transparent way.
- Have a clearly expressed and up-to-date APP privacy policy.
- The policy must include the kinds of personal information collected, how it's collected
  and held, the purposes of collection, use and disclosure, how an individual can access
  and correct their information, how they can complain, and whether the entity discloses
  personal information overseas (and to which countries where practicable).
- Make the policy available free of charge in an appropriate form (typically a web page).
- Make the policy available in a particular form on request.

## Evidence

- Published privacy policy URL, dated and version-controlled.
- Review cadence: typically annual or when collection practices change.
- Records of policy updates and the trigger for each.

## Where to look

- `/privacy`, `/privacy-policy`, `/legal/privacy` paths on web properties.
- Mobile app privacy disclosures (App Store / Play Store metadata).
- Onboarding flows that may include or link to the policy.
- Internal procedure for keeping the policy current.

## Common pitfalls

- Policy that has not been updated since the system was first launched.
- Generic policy copied from a template that does not describe the actual collection
  practices.
- Policy not findable (no footer link, no in-app link).
- Disclosures to overseas recipients omitted.
- Multiple policies on different properties of the same entity drifting apart.
