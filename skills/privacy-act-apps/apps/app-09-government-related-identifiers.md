# APP 9 - Adoption, use or disclosure of government related identifiers

APP 9 restricts organisations (not agencies) from adopting, using or disclosing
government related identifiers as their own identifier of an individual.

## In plain English

If you are a private-sector organisation, don't use a Medicare number, driver licence
number, Centrelink CRN or similar as your customer ID. Don't disclose them except for
specific reasons.

## Why it exists

Government identifiers are designed for specific government purposes. Using them as
general customer identifiers undermines that design, increases concentration risk, and
facilitates linkage of customer profiles across organisations.

## What APP 9 requires

- An organisation must not adopt a government related identifier of an individual as its
  own identifier of the individual, unless required or authorised by law.
- An organisation must not use or disclose a government related identifier unless one of
  the specific exceptions applies (necessary for the individual to verify identity for
  the organisation's activities, necessary for the organisation to fulfil a function
  required of it by an agency or state body, required or authorised by law, certain
  enforcement-related disclosures, or specific exceptions in the Regulations).

## Evidence

- Customer-identifier scheme documentation showing the organisation uses its own
  identifier.
- Storage of government identifiers separated from primary customer records, with
  documented exception basis for any case where the government identifier is required.
- Retention and destruction of government identifiers after the verification purpose is
  complete.

## Where to look

- Identity verification flows (driver licence, Medicare, passport).
- Loyalty / customer schemes using a government identifier as the key.
- Storage of identifier scans (photos of driver licences).
- Third-party identity verification providers and what they return.

## Common pitfalls

- Using a driver licence number as the primary customer ID.
- Retaining a photo of an ID after verification is complete, with no business need.
- Sharing identifier data with marketing partners.

## Cross-references

- APP 3 (collection): if you collect the identifier, you also need a basis.
- APP 11 (security): identifier data needs strong protection.
