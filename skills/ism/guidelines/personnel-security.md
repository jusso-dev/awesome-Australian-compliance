# Guidelines for Personnel Security

This guideline covers the cyber security aspects of how people are screened, trained,
managed and offboarded. Interlocks with PSPF Policies 12 to 14 (personnel security).

## In plain English

Know who has access. Train them. Have a process when they leave. Watch for insider risk.

## Why it exists

People are the path of least resistance for many incidents. Personnel controls reduce
both deliberate and accidental harm.

## Key topics

- Clearances appropriate to the classifications handled.
- Cyber security awareness training, including phishing-simulation.
- Acceptable use policies signed by users.
- Privileged user training tailored to admin responsibilities.
- Onboarding and offboarding procedures.
- Ongoing-suitability monitoring for users in higher-risk roles.
- Insider threat program.

## Where to look

- HR onboarding / offboarding workflows.
- Training platform records (annual training completion rates).
- Acceptable use policy library and signed acknowledgement records.
- Identity provider activity around terminations (timely revocation).
- Background check records for personnel with privileged access.

## Common evidence

- Training completion rate (target typically 100% before access granted, and on a
  recurring schedule).
- Sample of signed acceptable use policy acknowledgements.
- Timely access revocation evidence (typically same-day or next-business-day on
  termination).
- Clearance register for personnel handling PROTECTED information.

## Common pitfalls

- Offboarding handled by HR with no link to identity revocation.
- Long-tenured employees with cumulative access never reviewed.
- Annual training existing but not enforced (completion rate below 90% is a finding).
- No process for return of equipment on departure.
- Contractors with longer access windows than direct employees, slipping through annual
  reviews.
