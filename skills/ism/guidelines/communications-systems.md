# Guidelines for Communications Systems

This guideline covers fax, video conferencing and telephony from a security perspective.
The fax content remains in the ISM despite the technology's decline; the rest is the
modern unified communications stack.

## In plain English

Phones, video and fax carry sensitive content. Configure them with the same care as data
systems. Encrypt, control who can join, log what matters.

## Key topics

- Telephony and SIP infrastructure.
- Video conferencing platforms (Teams, Webex, Zoom, agency-deployed equivalents).
- Recording controls and retention.
- Fax (residual usage in some agencies).
- Mobile phone use in sensitive areas (separately covered in physical security).

## Where to look

- Conferencing platform admin settings.
- Recording retention configuration.
- Telephony provider contracts and call detail records.
- Policy for sensitive discussions over video.

## Common evidence

- Tenant configuration export for the conferencing platform.
- Sample of recording retention settings against policy.
- External-participant policies and lobby controls.

## Common pitfalls

- Recordings retained forever in OneDrive without classification or access control.
- External participants able to join sensitive meetings without lobby control.
- Telephony recording in jurisdictions with different consent rules.
- Fax confidentiality assumed where the device is a multi-function printer with a hard
  drive.
