# Guidelines for Enterprise Mobility

This guideline covers smartphones, tablets and laptops used outside the office, plus the
mobile device management (MDM) framework around them.

## In plain English

Devices that leave the building behave differently. Encrypt their disks. Enforce
authentication. Manage them centrally. Restrict the data they hold. Have a plan when one
is lost.

## Key topics

- Mobile device management (MDM) enrolment requirements.
- Device encryption at rest.
- Strong authentication and biometric considerations.
- Application allowlisting on mobile platforms.
- Containerisation of work data (Android Work Profile, iOS managed app config).
- Remote wipe.
- BYOD posture.
- Travelling-overseas guidance for higher classifications.

## Where to look

- Intune / Jamf / Workspace ONE policy exports.
- Compliance reports showing enrolment coverage.
- Loss / theft register and ticket history.
- BYOD acceptable use documentation.

## Common evidence

- Compliance dashboard from the MDM showing non-compliant devices investigated.
- Encryption-at-rest enforcement policy.
- Sample remote wipe execution record.
- BYOD policy signed by users.

## Common pitfalls

- BYOD allowed without containerisation, leading to mixed personal and work data.
- Lost-device tickets not closed with a confirmed wipe.
- iOS jailbreak / Android root detection disabled.
- MDM coverage excludes contractors and short-term staff.
- Travelling-overseas guidance silent on higher classifications.
