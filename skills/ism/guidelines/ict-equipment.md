# Guidelines for ICT Equipment

This guideline covers the lifecycle of ICT equipment: procurement, configuration,
maintenance, repair and disposal.

## In plain English

Track the equipment. Configure it before letting users near it. Maintain it on a
schedule. Dispose of it properly when retired.

## Key topics

- Asset inventory.
- Standard operating environment (SOE) for workstations and servers.
- Equipment maintenance contracts.
- Repairs handled with regard to classification of data on the device.
- Equipment reuse within and across classifications.
- Disposal and sanitisation (cryptographic erasure, physical destruction).

## Where to look

- CMDB / asset inventory.
- SOE build and deployment automation.
- Maintenance contracts with vendors.
- Disposal certificates from third-party destruction services.

## Common evidence

- Asset inventory completeness check (sample devices traceable to the inventory).
- SOE deployment proof for a sample device.
- Disposal certificates with serial numbers matching the inventory.

## Common pitfalls

- Asset inventory missing fleet devices, especially mobile and contractor laptops.
- Repairs sent to a vendor who does not have the right clearances for the data on the
  drive.
- Sanitisation done with file-deletion only, not cryptographic erasure or destruction.
- Old equipment "donated" without sanitisation.
