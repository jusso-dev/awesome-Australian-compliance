# PSPF Policy 12 - Eligibility and suitability of personnel

This policy covers initial assessment of personnel: eligibility for engagement,
suitability for the role, and the security clearance process where required.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

Before someone joins or takes on a role, check that they are eligible and suitable.
Where the role needs a clearance, complete the clearance process before granting access.

## Key requirements

- Assess eligibility (citizenship requirements, character checks).
- Assess suitability for the role (relevant background, integrity).
- Sponsor and obtain security clearances where required.
- Confirm clearance level matches the role's access requirements.

## Evidence

- HR onboarding records: identity verification, eligibility checks.
- Police checks and other background verification.
- Clearance application and approval records.
- Role-to-clearance mapping in the HR system.

## Where to look

- HR system.
- AGSVA (Australian Government Security Vetting Agency) clearance records.
- Position descriptions specifying clearance requirements.

## Common pitfalls

- Roles requiring a clearance left vacant or filled with conditional access pending the
  clearance.
- Eligibility checks completed at hire but not refreshed when the role changes.
- Contractor onboarding bypassing the same checks applied to direct employees.
