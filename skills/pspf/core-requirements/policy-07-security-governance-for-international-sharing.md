# PSPF Policy 7 - Security governance for international sharing

This policy covers sharing classified or sensitive information with foreign governments
and international organisations.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

When you share classified information with another country, do it under the right
agreement, with the right caveats, to the right recipient, with records.

## Key requirements

- Identify when information sharing engages this policy (sharing classified information
  outside Australia, sharing with foreign governments or international bodies).
- Operate under appropriate intergovernmental security arrangements.
- Mark, transmit and store the information consistently with the receiving party's
  obligations.
- Maintain records of disclosure.

## Evidence

- Intergovernmental security agreement (or equivalent) for the relationship.
- Records of disclosure.
- Recipient's clearance and need-to-know basis.

## Where to look

- International cooperation arrangements held by the agency.
- Records of formal disclosures of classified information.

## Common pitfalls

- Ad-hoc sharing outside the formal framework.
- Loss of caveat propagation when the information transits multiple systems.
- Lack of records when senior personnel disclose information in person to foreign
  counterparts.
