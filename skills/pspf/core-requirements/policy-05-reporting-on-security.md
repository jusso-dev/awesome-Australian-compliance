# PSPF Policy 5 - Reporting on security

This policy requires entities to report on their protective security performance,
including the annual PSPF report to the Attorney-General's Department, the relevant
Minister, and ASIO where relevant.

Note: reporting format and recipients may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

Report on your security performance annually. Cover what you did well, what you did
not, and what you plan to fix.

## Key requirements

- Provide an annual PSPF report to AGD covering maturity, significant incidents and
  uplift planned.
- Report security incidents to relevant bodies (AGD, ASIO, ACSC) as required.
- Report internally to the accountable authority and relevant committees on a defined
  cadence.

## Evidence

- Annual PSPF report submission.
- Incident reporting records.
- Internal security reports to executive and audit committees.

## Where to look

- Document management system entry for the annual report.
- Incident ticketing system for incident-reporting actions.
- Executive committee minutes covering security.

## Common pitfalls

- Annual report submitted late or incomplete.
- Significant incidents not included for reputational reasons.
- Internal reporting cadence not regular enough to inform decision-making.
