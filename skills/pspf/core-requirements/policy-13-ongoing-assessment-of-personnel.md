# PSPF Policy 13 - Ongoing assessment of personnel

This policy covers ongoing monitoring of personnel after engagement: clearance
maintenance, role changes, conduct issues and the response when a person's
circumstances change.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

People change. So do the circumstances around them. Recheck on a schedule and react
to events that affect suitability.

## Key requirements

- Periodic revalidation of clearances at the cadence set by AGSVA.
- Process for reporting and responding to changes in personal circumstances that
  affect suitability (financial difficulty, foreign contacts, conduct issues).
- Insider threat awareness and response.
- Annual confirmation that clearance holders still need their current clearance level.

## Evidence

- Revalidation schedule with completion records.
- Reporting channel for changes in circumstances (anonymous or named).
- Insider threat programme records.
- Annual clearance recertification records.

## Where to look

- Clearance management records.
- HR case management for conduct issues.
- Insider threat tooling and the operating playbook.

## Common pitfalls

- Revalidation past due, with continued access granted.
- Reporting channel exists but is never used because employees do not know it.
- Insider threat treated as a security-team-only concern.
- Clearance level not stepped down when the role no longer requires it.
