# PSPF Policy 4 - Security maturity monitoring

This policy requires entities to monitor and report on their security maturity.

Note: the maturity model itself was revised in the PSPF 2024 redesign. Confirm against
the live release.

## In plain English

Measure where you are. Track whether you are improving. Be honest about it.

## Key requirements

- Self-assess maturity against the PSPF maturity model.
- Track maturity over time.
- Identify areas where maturity is below the agency's risk tolerance.
- Build maturity uplift into the security plan and budget cycle.

## Evidence

- Maturity self-assessment with supporting evidence per assessed area.
- Trend report over multiple assessment cycles.
- Maturity uplift plan with milestones and owners.
- Independent review where the agency has elected one.

## Where to look

- Annual PSPF self-assessment workpapers.
- Internal audit reports covering security.
- Capability uplift programmes funded through the security plan.

## Common pitfalls

- Maturity self-rated against the desired state rather than current evidence.
- Maturity scores stable for years with no underlying change.
- Improvement programmes that do not translate to measurable maturity uplift.
