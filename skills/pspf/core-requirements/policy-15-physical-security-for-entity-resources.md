# PSPF Policy 15 - Physical security for entity resources

This policy covers the physical security of the agency's people, information and assets.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

Protect facilities, equipment and people. Apply security zones. Control entry. Monitor.

## Key requirements

- Apply protective security zones to facilities.
- Implement entry controls (badge, biometrics, escort) appropriate to the zone.
- Protect ICT equipment and removable media in line with their classification.
- Plan for security in the event of disruption (evacuation, denial of service to
  facilities, severe weather).

## Evidence

- Zone designations for facilities.
- Entry control system configuration and logs.
- CCTV coverage maps.
- Security guard arrangements.
- Disruption plans (continuity, evacuation).

## Where to look

- Facilities management documentation.
- Physical security risk assessments.
- Building access logs.

## Common pitfalls

- Zone model on paper but doors held open by tape.
- CCTV without monitoring or retention.
- Shared tenancy buildings without zone separation for agency space.
- Disruption plans not exercised.
