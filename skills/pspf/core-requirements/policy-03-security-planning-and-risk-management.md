# PSPF Policy 3 - Security planning and risk management

This policy requires entities to plan their protective security through formal risk
management.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

Identify protective security risks. Decide how to treat them. Document the plan. Keep
it current.

## Key requirements

- Establish a security risk management framework consistent with AS / NZS ISO 31000 or
  equivalent.
- Identify, analyse and treat protective security risks across people, information and
  physical security.
- Document the agency security plan.
- Review the plan on a defined cadence (typically annual) and after material change.

## Evidence

- Agency security plan, current and approved.
- Security risk register.
- Threat assessment.
- Risk treatment plan with named owners and due dates.
- Records of plan review.

## Where to look

- Agency security plan repository.
- Enterprise risk management system.
- Threat assessment documents from internal or external advisers.

## Common pitfalls

- Plan written once and not refreshed.
- Risk register dominated by IT risks with personnel and physical risks under-represented.
- No documented link from risks to security treatments.
- Treatment plan owned by the security team alone with no business-line accountability.
