# PSPF Policy 14 - Separating personnel

This policy governs the security implications when personnel leave the agency or move
into a different role.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

When someone leaves, recover their access, their assets and their obligations. Make
sure the process actually runs end to end.

## Key requirements

- Timely revocation of physical and logical access on separation.
- Return of equipment, identity cards, classified material.
- Exit interview where appropriate.
- Continuing obligations regarding classified information communicated at departure.

## Evidence

- Offboarding workflow with security tasks: access revocation, equipment return,
  clearance update.
- Records of timely access revocation (same-day or next-business-day for most cases).
- Asset recovery records.
- Departure briefing records for clearance holders.

## Where to look

- HR offboarding tools.
- IAM and IdP for access revocation.
- Asset management for equipment recovery.
- Clearance management for stepping the clearance down.

## Common pitfalls

- Access revocation lagging behind employment termination.
- Contractor offboarding handled inconsistently with employee offboarding.
- Equipment marked returned in the asset system but never physically recovered.
- Departure briefing missing for clearance holders, leading to confusion about
  continuing obligations.
