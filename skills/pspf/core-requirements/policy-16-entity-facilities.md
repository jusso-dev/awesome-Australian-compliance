# PSPF Policy 16 - Entity facilities

This policy specifies certification requirements for facilities holding classified
information.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign.

## In plain English

Buildings or rooms holding classified material must meet certification requirements
appropriate to the classification.

## Key requirements

- Facilities holding PROTECTED and above must meet certified Zone standards.
- Certification processes engaged before classified material is introduced.
- Recertification on a defined cadence and on material change.
- Specific arrangements for shared tenancy and visitor access.

## Evidence

- Zone certification documents for each facility.
- Recertification cycle calendar.
- Visitor access logs.
- Shared-tenancy arrangements documented and risk-assessed.

## Where to look

- Facilities management records.
- Property leases and certifications.
- Annual security inspection records.

## Common pitfalls

- Certification done at fit-out and never refreshed.
- Co-location with non-agency staff in PROTECTED-rated zones without segregation.
- Visitor access patterns not aligned with zone restrictions.
