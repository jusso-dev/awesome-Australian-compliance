# PSPF core requirements index

The PSPF organises its requirements into policies. The pre-2024 structure used 16
numbered policies grouped under four outcomes. The 2024 redesign reorganised this set;
confirm against the live PSPF on protectivesecurity.gov.au and update this index when
the live structure changes.

## Outcomes (PSPF pre-2024 structure)

- **Security governance**: Policies 1 to 7.
- **Information security**: Policies 8 to 11.
- **Personnel security**: Policies 12 to 14.
- **Physical security**: Policies 15 and 16.

## Using these pages

- For an agency security plan, walk every policy applicable to the agency's
  classifications and operations.
- For an annual PSPF report, use the maturity model from the live PSPF release; the
  pages here describe topics rather than scoring criteria.
- For audits, focus on the outcome the policy supports rather than just the policy
  number.

## Note on the 2024 redesign

The 2024 redesign rearranged the policy set and updated the maturity model. Many
agency artefacts still reference the pre-2024 numbers. When mapping, confirm against
the live release at the time of the work. See [`../versions.md`](../versions.md).
