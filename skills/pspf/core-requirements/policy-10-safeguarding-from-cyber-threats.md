# PSPF Policy 10 - Safeguarding information from cyber threats

This policy is the PSPF's anchor for cyber security. It defers detailed technical
controls to the ACSC's Information Security Manual.

Note: numbering and content may have shifted in the PSPF 2024 redesign. This policy
historically also covered Essential Eight expectations.

## In plain English

Implement cyber controls consistent with the ISM. Implement the Essential Eight at the
maturity level appropriate to your risk tolerance.

## Key requirements

- Implement the Essential Eight mitigation strategies.
- Apply ISM controls applicable to the agency's classifications.
- Maintain accreditation / authorisation of systems holding classified information,
  including consideration of IRAP assessments.
- Govern cyber risk through the agency security plan.

## Evidence

- System Security Plan and Statement of Applicability for each system.
- IRAP outputs where applicable.
- Essential Eight assessment results.
- Risk acceptance records.

## Where to look

- ISM-aligned SSPs and SoAs.
- ACSC Essential Eight Assessment Process Guide outputs.
- Risk register entries against unimplemented controls.

## Common pitfalls

- Essential Eight target Maturity Level not aligned with the agency risk tolerance.
- Authorisation decisions made without a current IRAP.
- ISM controls implemented in isolation from the broader PSPF.
